Aureus Trial Booking:

Simplifying trial lesson bookings for aspiring musicians.

Introduction

Aureus Academy is one of Singapore’s leading music schools, offering one-on-one music lessons across more than 30 locations. Before enrolling, prospective students can sign up for a trial lesson to explore different instruments and learning styles.

The existing trial booking process relied heavily on manual staff coordination, requiring back-and-forth communication to confirm lesson details, recommend teachers, and schedule sessions. This created operational inefficiencies and increased friction for prospective customers.

The Aureus Trial Booking revamp aimed to streamline the trial sign-up experience through a guided, self-service booking flow - empowering customers to complete bookings independently while reducing manual effort for staff.

My Role

I led the end-to-end design of the trial booking experience, collaborating closely with the operations, front-end and back-end teams.

  • Gathered business and operational requirements
  • Mapped out user journeys and flows
  • Aligned with stakeholders to identify and handle technical and operational constraints

Key Objectives

Before redesigning the experience, I worked closely with the operations team to understand existing pain points and customer behaviour.

    The project focused on three key goals:
  1. Reduce form abandonment
    Customers often dropped off during manual follow-ups or delayed communication. The booking process needed to feel lightweight, clear, and easy to complete.
  2. Reduce manual workload for staff
    Trial sign-ups previously required significant staff involvement to coordinate schedules and answer repetitive questions. The goal was to create a more self-service process.
  3. Improve conversion from trial to enrolment
    The booking flow needed to do more than collect information - it had to help prospective students feel confident about taking the next step.

Since the majority of traffic came from mobile devices, a mobile-first approach was prioritised from the outset.

Establishing Lead Source Visibility

A new lead source capture system was introduced during sign-up to identify how customers discovered Aureus. By improving visibility into acquisition sources, the business could better understand customer behaviour and minimise potential drop-offs.

Matching Students to the Right Teachers

One key friction point in the previous process was uncertainty around teacher selection. To increase confidence, the booking experience introduced teacher recommendations based on student preferences, such as instrument, proficiency level, and location. Instead of assigning teachers manually behind the scenes, customers could better understand who they would be learning from before confirming a trial. This created a more personalised experience while helping prospective students feel more comfortable committing to lessons.

Impact & Conclusion

Key improvements following the redesign included a clearer, mobile-friendly booking journey, reduced manual effort spent on trial coordination, and improved visibility into lead sources and customer intent

The project continues to evolve through ongoing iteration and operational feedback.
